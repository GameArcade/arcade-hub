How to Play

1
Slide the tiles in any direction using arrow keys, swipe gestures, or the on-screen buttons.
▲
◀
▼
▶
2
Merge matching tiles. When two tiles with the same number collide, they combine into one!
2 + 2 → 4
3
Build up to 2048! Keep merging to create higher numbers. Reach the 2048 tile to win — then keep going for an even higher score!
4
Don't get stuck. After every move, a new tile appears. If the board fills up and no merges are possible, the game is over.
5
Tips: Keep your highest tile in a corner. Build a chain of descending values along one edge. Think ahead!
