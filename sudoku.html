How to Play

1
Fill the grid so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.
2
Tap a cell to select it, then tap a number to place it. Pre-filled (given) numbers can't be changed.
3
Notes mode ✏ — toggle it to write small candidate numbers in a cell. Tap a number again to remove the note.
4
Hints 💡 — stuck? Use a hint to reveal the correct number. You get 3 hints per game.
5
Undo ↩ — made a mistake? Undo your last moves as many times as you need.
6
Difficulty — Easy has more given numbers, Expert has fewer. Pick your challenge!
